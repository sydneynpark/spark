# May 17 Field Notes

A productive morning at the reservoir. Five species photographed before 1:30 pm.

## American White Pelican

The highlight of the day — a lone pelican working the shallows at the north end. These birds
look almost prehistoric up close. Wingspan over nine feet, bright orange bill pouch in full
breeding color.

`photo://2025/05/17/2025-05-17 074801 - American White Pelican.jpg`

## Purple Martin

A small colony has taken up residence in the gourd rack near the boat launch. Caught this one
perched briefly between sorties.

`photo://2025/05/17/2025-05-17 092311 - Purple Martin.jpg`

## Yellow-headed Blackbird

One of my favorites. The yellow head against the black body is almost absurdly bold. This one
was singing from a cattail stem at the marsh edge.

`photo://2025/05/17/2025-05-17 123115 - Yellow-headed Blackbird.jpg`
