# May 23 Field Notes

A productive morning at the marsh. A few highlights:

## Pied-billed Grebe

A grebe in full breeding plumage, black band across its bill, diving and resurfacing in the
open water past the reeds. It never stayed up for more than a few seconds at a time.

`photo://birds/2026/05/23/2026-05-23 - 864A7759.jpg`

## Snowy Egret

Several egrets were working the shallows along the channel. Black bill, black legs, and those
bright yellow feet, which they stir through the mud to flush out prey.

`photo://birds/2026/05/23/2026-05-23 - 864A8210.jpg`

## Common Yellowthroat

One of my favorites. This one sang from the top of a shrub at the marsh edge, bandit mask and
all, and stayed put just long enough.

`photo://birds/2026/05/23/2026-05-23 - 864A8618-2.jpg`
