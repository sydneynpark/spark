# Hello World

Welcome to the first post on spark.wiki! This is a place where I'll write about birding, photography, and whatever else catches my interest.

## Getting Started

I've been birding for a few years now, but only recently started photographing the birds I see. There's something different about trying to *capture* a bird versus just watching it — you end up paying much closer attention to the light, the background, and the behavior.

## What to Expect

- Photography technique and gear

- Trip reports
