## Commentary

### 20%
The narrator's voice is so gentle and trusting that the first hint something
is wrong lands like a gut punch.

### 70%
The reveal of who Piranesi actually is recontextualizes everything before it.
Went back and reread the opening immediately after finishing this section.

### Overall
Short, strange, and quietly devastating. One of my favorite reads this year.
