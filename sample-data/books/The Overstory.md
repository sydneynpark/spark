## Commentary

### 10%
Each character's origin story is basically a self-contained short story —
Patricia Westerford's chapter alone is worth the price of admission.

### 55%
Once the threads start converging, the pacing picks up considerably.

### 95%
The ending pulls every thread together thematically even where the plots
don't literally intersect. Didn't expect to be moved this much by a book
partly about tree biology.

### Overall
Slow to start, but it earns its length. I'll be thinking about this one
for a while.
