## Commentary
### Overall
The immersion into the tense, murky atmosphere of memories being pieced together after the fact was addictive. I finished the book in a day, hence the grade of 10 on intrigue. My heart raced in moments of danger and I was nearly sent into a panic. In a way, it was even more torturous that the danger was never realized onscreen. 

When the dots started to connect, I found myself underwhelmed. There were pieces that seemed nonsensical. The entire saga of the sister simply didn't fit with the villain's motives. And what's the deal with the final reveal? I wouldn't think an obsessive stalker would be satisfied with a proxy. 

Was it a literary masterpiece? Certainly not. But I think I'll remember this book and the feelings it gave me for a long time.
