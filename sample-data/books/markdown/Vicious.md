## Commentary
### Overall
I read this on a recommendation from my brother in law after complaining about Addie LaRue. He said Vicious is way better. I had heard that elsewhere too, that Addie LaRue was actually her worst book and nothing like any of her other books. I suppose it was a little different, but it felt so hollow. The characters kept commenting about how the undead people were soooo evil, but the only way they ever backed that up was by saying things like "Look at those dead eyes." "Your eyes look dead." "I can just see in your eyes that you're not right." Like, okay? How about giving me an example of something they have done that's evil? Or even enlightening us to a thought process they have that demonstrates their evilness? No, instead the author just tells us the people are evil and we should hate them.
