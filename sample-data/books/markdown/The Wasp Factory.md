## Commentary
### Overall
In the beginning and middle I really enjoyed living inside Frank's head. The way he laid out his sterile logic and emotionless moral compass let me see the world from his eyes. It's always a neat experience when an author can really place you inside a mind that works differently. 

But when I finished the book, I was left confused and a little disappointed. I just didn't see the point of the reveal in the final pages. Honestly, the book would have been more impactful if there was no concrete explanation for why Frank is the way he is. And for the reveal to mean something, I wish we could have spent more than 30 seconds reflecting on it. It was like the book said, "Oh by the way, here's a huge twist, the end!"

I also didn't see the point of Eric's story happening in parallel. Especially his exposition, which I did not feel fully explained how he became the character we know. There *have* to be some missing pieces. The Eric we know is a caricature with no redeeming characteristics and no reason for me to care about him. So what was supposed to be an emotionally charged reunion fell flat for me.
