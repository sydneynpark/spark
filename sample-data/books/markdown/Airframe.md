## Commentary
### Overall
This is my second Michael Crichton book. In both books, I felt they had a little too much technical detail that maybe wasn't important to the story. There were times I simply wished he'd get on with the book. I let him get away with it in *Congo* because the suspense was so high - here, I did not have the same emotional buy-in. Although, in *Airframe*, the technical details were more relevant to the story. Perhaps it simply was not for me.

I will say, I loved what the answer to the mystery ended up being. I did not see it coming but it made total sense. It was a nice little bamboozle.
