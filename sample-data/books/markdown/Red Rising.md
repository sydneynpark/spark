## Commentary
### Overall
I'm not typically into the scifi/fantasy sagas where there's a whole huge political landscape I have to memorize and a massive cast of characters to keep track of. But I enjoyed this more than I thought I would. It never had the pull where I spend all day itching to get back to reading, but when I had the discipline to open it, I was richly rewarded. Red Rising strikes a good balance of politics, action, and interpersonal. I need a refresher with some books that come more naturally to me, but I would come back and pick up the next in the series.
