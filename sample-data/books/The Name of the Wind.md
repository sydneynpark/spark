## Commentary

### 50%
Kvothe's time at the University is charming but starts to feel aimless
around here — I found myself wanting the frame story to intrude more often.

### Overall
Beautifully written and easy to sink into, even when the plot itself
wanders. Frustrating mainly because the frame narrative promises so much
that this first book doesn't deliver on yet.
