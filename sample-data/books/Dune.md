## Commentary

### 8%
The opening chapters throw a lot of terminology at once. Worth pushing through.

### 40%
Paul's transformation after Arrakis starts to feel inevitable rather than
surprising — I think that's intentional.

### 90%
The ending is more of a beginning. Glad I already have the sequel queued up.

### Overall
Dense but rewarding. This is one where a second read will probably reveal more
than the first.
